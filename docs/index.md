  # Woof the AI native photo gallery


  OuEstCharlie Woof is the photo and video gallery companion to your AI assistant
  (Claude Desktop, Goose, VS Code / GitHub Copilot...). Your photos and videos remain
  exactly where they are — on your own drives (local or mounted).


  No cloud subscription. No proprietary lock-in. Your library, your way.


  **Try Woof today — see [how to install and take your first steps](/2026/04/01/ouestcharlie-woof-install-first-steps/).**

Woof never touches your original files and works with the folders you already have. Descriptions, tags and ratings are kept in standard XMP sidecars next to your photos, readable by Lightroom and darktable, so your organisation outlives any app ([why it matters](/2026/04/10/why-we-need-to-move-past-gallery-apps/)). Search by description, tags, date, place or camera, browse in a grid or full screen, and let [AI workflows sort and caption your photos](/2026/08/26/ai-workflow-sort-photos-by-grouping-clusters/). Free and open source (MIT).
